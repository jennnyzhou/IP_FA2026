genAI as creative material

Chinese Ladies Looking at European Curiosities (1868)

For this assignment, I decided to use Chinese Ladies Looking at European Curiosities, by John Evan Hodgson (1831-1895). The painting is a painting depicting a Chinese man with European objects, and three Chinese women looking at him with enthusiasm. I thought the composition in itself was interesting, and I wanted to experiment and see how AI would interpret the description of a scene, specifically being a Victorian painting depicting Chinese people. With each "main" picture, I added the prompt I gave each AI model, which were ChatGPT, Firefly, and Gemini. I was very surprised with the results.



By the time I got to altering the image, I would kind of just act instinctively on ideas, the first one being the Jester meme, as I thought it was funny to have the figure on the left be a jester, and the figure on the right be a queen, so I gave them a jester hat and a tiara, as well as a throne.



I thought I was not adding any information on ethnicity, age, gender, etc. so I was surprised when (especially by the second iteration), all three models returned Asian people; I'm curious to know whether this was because of stored information in each model, which Gemini doesn't seem so to do without an account, or was it the clothing description that alluded to Asian figures?

I feel like for the third image, I wasn't as surprised ChatGPT returned an Asian lady, as I did use my personal account here, and perhaps it is based on information it already knows about me (I could be making things up). I did make sure that the third description was the most vague just so I could get either more variety or more unexpected results, and it was interesting to see Firefly and Gemini returned brunette white ladies.

I feel like all four rounds of generated images were lacking in style, although now I realized I didn't intentionally include a specific painting style, as I felt like it didn't come across so clearly in the first image. I think I was referencing the painting style in the assignment reference, perhaps expecting that kind of style?

To an extent it was fun to have some sort of choppy composition to feed into AI to make into an actual cohesive image, but I still feel like they all look so ... soulless to an extent. This was similar to what I was doing in my last job, where I was constantly having to make quick compositions on Illustrator before feeding it to Gemini for a "final" product or mockup. During that time, I felt (and still feel) like although it does offer quick solutions and products, it doesn't feel the same as an actual painting, or an actual human produced photograph.
