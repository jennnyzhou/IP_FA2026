jenny's time capsule

↑ back to top

i chose this object as it is one of my few group photos with my group of friends from back home, despite us all knowing each other for nearly 20 years. this was i think our last group picture before i left for new york, so i printed it out as a polaroid and have had it in a tiny frame by my bedside all of these years. friends isn't even an adequate word for them but if i try to put it into words i will sound so corny.

↑ back to top

i feel like collecting jerseys has to be an argentine experience; having football be such a big big part of our culture naturally continuously has made the stack in my closet bigger without even realizing it. i hold these close because it connects me back to home, and we can recognize each other through our clothing.

↑ back to top

going through my cabinets and belongings i found the stack in which i keep letters. what a privilege it is to be loved!!! these are so so special because i think handwritten letters are so intimate, and to have my friends and family take the time to dedicate this practice to me makes me feel so seen. to have my cousins all collectively write me a letter as i was going through a hard time, or a friend taking the time to send me a letter from the military in taiwan make me so grateful and shape who i am.

↑ back to top

a bit less intimate but similar to my jerseys, my rooommate and i have been close friends since seventh grade in argentina, and each time we go back or someone visits us, they bring some of our favourite snacks so we can enjoy them and have pieces of home in our living room. we have a little cabinet under our coffee table with these treats :D

↑ back to top

although i live flights and continents from my family and friends, i truly have the privilege to still be able to go home once or twice a year, as well as to travel. this collection of plane tickets through the years hold so many memories of travelling to see the world, or visiting / adventuring with friends and family and i think these experience shape how i view the world and myself and constantly open my eyes to new things.
