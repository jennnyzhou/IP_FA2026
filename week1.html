50 ways of seeing!

After visiting the New York Transit Museum, I decided to use an iconic object as the core of this assignment: the MetroCard. When I sat down to start, I somehow just decided to open the Photo Booth app to try to play with how I can make the letters with my hands, which then turned to my body, arms, and face. In between some of these iterations, I also paused to do what I usually start with — sketching and drawing, so I started with drawing the different angles using different tools (pencil, pen, marker, etc.)

I would love to say I didn't start going a bit crazy by iteration 20 but I think that really pushed me to think even harder about how else I can depict the MetroCard. But with perfect timing my hunger striked and food became my next medium; I tried to write MetroCard with mayonnaise on my toast while I waited for my eggs to boil, and although it is illegible, I kind of really like how it looks. Then after playing around some more, I went on a walk to the art supply store, with no specific idea of what I wanted to get or do. Once I walked in, stickers and clay caught my attention, so I took those home with me and started to make things, which is when things started to get more abstract.

I started playing around with clay, and thought about the MetroCard as a theme or concept rather than a physical object, which is when I made a clay cardholder, a fridge magnet, stars, keychains, etc. I also tried playing with star stickers and compositions, some more successful than others.

I think my main realization was that at first, although I was being playful with my mediums, the end goal was always to recreate the MetroCard in one way or another, but as the number of iterations increased, I started to think more abstractly and created things that were far away from what the MetroCard physically looks like. I also made the composition of the images random as I feel like even from the beginning, I was jumping between digital and sketching, and there was no proper linear process to my iterations, just new ideas and coming back to old ones.
